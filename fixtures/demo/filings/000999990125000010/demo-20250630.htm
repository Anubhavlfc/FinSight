PART I

Item 1. Business

DEMO Analytics Corp (SYNTHETIC) develops analytics software. We report two segments: Cloud Analytics, which sells subscriptions to our hosted platform, and On-Premises Licenses, which sells perpetual licenses and related support.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our results depend on renewals of Cloud Analytics subscriptions. We are expanding data centre capacity, which requires significant capital expenditure.

PART II

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

Fiscal year 2025 compared with fiscal year 2024.

Revenue

Revenue increased $180 million or 18% to $1,180 million, driven primarily by growth in Cloud Analytics subscriptions, which increased $195 million as new enterprise customers adopted the platform. On-Premises License revenue decreased $15 million as customers continued to migrate to Cloud Analytics.

Operating income

Operating income increased $80 million or 53% to $230 million. Operating margin improved because revenue grew faster than operating expenses. Research and development expenses increased $15 million, driven by headcount growth in our cloud engineering teams. Selling and marketing expenses increased $20 million, reflecting higher sales commissions on Cloud Analytics bookings.

Net income

Net income was $150 million in fiscal year 2025 compared with a net loss of $20 million in fiscal year 2024. The prior-year net loss reflected a $135 million charge for the settlement of patent litigation that did not recur in fiscal year 2025.

Liquidity and capital resources

Cash flows from operating activities were $260 million in fiscal year 2025 and $180 million in fiscal year 2024. Additions to property and equipment were $90 million, up from $60 million, primarily reflecting the first phase of our data centre expansion.
